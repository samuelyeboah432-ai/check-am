Is this message a scam? Check am before you send money.

Paste a suspicious SMS, WhatsApp message or investment offer. You get a risk level, the scam type and the exact warning signs, in plain language.

Names the scam

Reversal, fake promo, Ponzi, fake job and phishing patterns common in Ghana.

Shows the red flags

Every verdict points to the words that raised the alarm, so you learn the pattern.

Never says “100% safe”

Even a low score keeps a caution. A false “safe” is how people lose money.

Prototype by Samuel Kwame Yeboah. This demo uses a rule-based pattern library. The full product adds AI analysis and a community scam-report database, and offers a screening API to fintechs and MFIs.
